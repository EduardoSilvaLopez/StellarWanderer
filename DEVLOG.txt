### Edition tricks

Fold Level 2 (Ctrl+K Ctrl+2) collapses every method body, so the class turns into a list of signatures.
Fold All Regions (Ctrl+K Ctrl+8)
Go to Symbol in File (Ctrl+Shift+O) lists the methods, and typing : groups them by kind.

GIANT_SUN_GAIN sets the brightness.
GIANT_LIGHT_PENETRATION (now 0.15) sets how quickly the light dies with depth.
GIANT_LIMB_DARKENING sets the darker edge.
JovianWorld.MASS_EXTINCTION sets how opaque the clouds are.
