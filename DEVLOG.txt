### Past of the NPCs

NPCs are generated in steps and from different "crawling" mechanisms following a lazy-loading strategy.

What we have no is the "generation from settlement": We give a current role and we generate from there.

Lazy generation of the past life, when asked about life or family.
- When they finished their studies, which depends on the role: at age 16 or 22 with a small random factor.
- Time keeping the role, which depends on how adequate it is for this person.
- Gap between roles, which depend on loyalty (more loyalty, less gap)
- Time of retirement, which depends on loyalty, adequacy and longevity
- Time of death, which depends on longevity.
- Number of relationships, which depends on sexuality.
- Each individual relationship, with uniform probability and the change to overlap.

Lazy generation of friends, when asked about friends. Requires basic generation.
- Friendships, which can be ex- or different ones.
