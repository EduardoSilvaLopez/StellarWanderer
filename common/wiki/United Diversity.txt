This is the biggest political entity of the setting, with 50 stellar systems with cities in the year 700.

It includes the Moon, the most populated world by far.

Its most important political principles are:
* Human rights, including a Universal Basic Income, basic universal healthcare and the ban of slavery.
* Technocracy: Vote is given for a time period to those who have serviced the State recently and proved some political
knowledge. Expertise is acknowledged, and science is cherished.
* Militarism: The military, conceived ONLY as a protection against the extraterrestrial threat (NEVER against other
humans) is highly respected and well paid.
* Federalism: Cultural diversity is encouraged, to the point that they consider other political entities as interesting
experiments in this direction more than as competition.
* Capitalism: It is seen as an important engine of prosperity and growth, which must however be controlled to avoid
a plutocracy. Nonetheless, producing something society is ready to purchase is considered a clear personal merit, and
so wealthy people are admired and entitled to live in luxury.
* Democracy: Once a citizen has the right to vote, different institutions ensure their ability to affect political life.

United Diversity is the undisputed economic powerhouse of mankind, has the largest armed forces and the most advanced
science. However, it is only second in industrial power (after Those-Who-Share) and punches below its weight in
relation to soft power and cultural production (compared to The Shining Throne).

Points which are critisized overseas and also by their own rebels:
* The living conditions of those who only have Universal Basic Income. Many live in cells inside standardized buildings
with common spaces for cooking and common washrooms, getting a very small direct economic help. The universal
healthcare is very limited. Ghettos do exist.
* A perceived obsession with money across the society.
* A perceived obsession with the military, certain cult of violence and war, a focus on the horrible war against the
extraterrestrials two hundred years ago.
* The political power of the very wealthy, the corruption produced by this.
* The myriad of different communities and groups, often at odds with each other, which makes common projects difficult.
