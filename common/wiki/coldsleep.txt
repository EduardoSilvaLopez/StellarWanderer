To make the passage of time more bearable, the pilot's veins are connected to a system which can deliver
drugs that make subjective time pass faster. The ship's clock shows the factor by which subjective time is
accelerated, also called "time compression". The ship detects that the pilot's reaction time is slowed, so it
prevents some actions, such as opening the scoop. This state of the pilot is called "coldsleep", and the
technology used is the same as for hibernation during interstellar travel without portals.
