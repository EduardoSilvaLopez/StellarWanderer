In the year 0 a huge beam of high-energy radiation hit the Earth, killing not only almost all humans but also every living organism besides some radioactivity-resistant bacteria and deep sea unicellular organisms. The Earth was transformed into a radioactive wasteland.

People called it "the Disaster", the time before "Pre-Disaster", PreDis for short. From the year 0 on, it is "Post-Disaster", PosDis for short. However, it soon became clear that it was an intentional attack by some ETs (extraterrestrials), sent from outer space long ago. There is still dispute about how long ago, but at least centuries.

Only two settlements on the Moon survived, collecting survivors from orbital bases and spaceships on transit. The first base was led by a European Union / USA consortium with Japanese collaboration. The other, smaller, by China. Citizens of other countries survived there too, but many countries did not have survivors of their own, especially poor ones and those which were  geopolitically isolated at that moment, including Russia, North Korea and Israel.

All languages except English stopped being used in normal life about 100 PosDis, but they are kept for special purposes - particularly naming nationwide-relevant things - and as second language in some communities - particularly naming people. Names of people in general are proudly kept from the past, as a way to honour Earth, all over the human space, a practice approved for different reasons by all important ideologies and political entities.

From 400 on, a Return movement emerged, with the intention to make the recovery and repopulation of Earth the first priority, and then make it again the centre of civilisation. Even with some victories, taking over the Moon Central Administration and renaming it the Earthean Central Government, it loses popularity and by 450 is largely politically irrelevant (not culturally, though). The successors do not rename back the government, nor the Earthean Defence Forces.

In the year 500 PosDis, mankind had extended over the whole solar system besides Earth, which was scavenged for loot but not yet settled again, as radioactivity levels were still high. In this year (even if mankind discovered one year later) a huge robotic spaceship, later called the Exterminating Mother, arrived and started producing lesser ships which would attack mankind. At the same time, it started building a stellar Portal to travel to another system by bending space with a wormhole.

From 501-513 a horrible war ensued, in which mankind first had to learn to escape, then resist the attacks, then sabotage the collection of resources (unknowingly sabotaging the construction of the Portal) and finally counter-attack, up to the destruction of the Exterminating Mother.

After that, mankind learned from the alien technology. They learned to make their own Portals, which require travelling conventionally with one part of the Portal while the other one remains in place. They started repopulating the Earth and healing it.

From 540 on, mankind started expanding through the stars in new ships which could ignore Relativity as they also bent the space around them.

Around 560, the Founders established the first system of Those-Who-Share (sometimes called THS).

In 580, The Shining Throne (sometimes called TST) was founded by an oligarch. She was a member of the Return movement, disappointed by the government's refusal to give Earth more priority, and decided to create her own "new centre of mankind".

The two main political forces of the time around 500, which were the Federalists and the Militarists, merged in a new consensus, calling the remaining space United Diversity, officially founded around 590.

United Diversity is the uncontested biggest power. However, it is also the most divided. It is losing its grip on its "de-jure" territory. The Return movement still exists. Many systems within now try new forms of society. Violent conflicts have happened far away from the Solar System. Some think UniDiv, as it is informally called, is about to disintegrate. A "New Militarist" movement tries to convince the politicians to open themselves to the possibility of using the Earthean Defence Forces (which are exclusively designed and funded to protect against an eventual return of the aliens) for human conflicts, something traditional Militarists would have considered absolutely evil.

In this world, an unremarkable Pilot ends training on the borders of new THS territory. He is supposed to spend years safely planting mines, extracting valuable minerals and getting a reputation for lonesome, hard work before retirement.

That Pilot may have other plans, though.
