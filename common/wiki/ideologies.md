# IDEOLOGIES
By order of appearance.

## The Common Base
This ideology did not have any name until historians later identified it. It is simply composed by a consensus which emerged from the population of the survivors after the Disaster, and which was largely due to their common situation, education and goals.

It is rational. Religion had few to no influence and rapidly disappeared, other supersticions were identified and efforts were made to eliminate them.

It is democratic, but in a wide sense. Power derives from the people. However, how this is express can take different forms.

It is fiercly proud of mankind, to point of being highly suspicious to ideas like superhumans, AI and modified animals. And blatantly paranoid towards extraterrestrials, for obvious reasons.

It is quite non-discriminatory: sexuality, age and ethnical group lose a lot of its relevance. Culture remains somehow important, though, and so does religion, the ideology being hostile to all of them.

It questions tradictional PreDis concepts of family, searching for a new model.

## Proto-Surnemalism
Also not named in its time, this was the vague idea that children born by artificial insemination, were problematic in some way, and should not hold positions of high responsibility. People following this kept close track of their surnames - two of them, by mother and by father.

This emerged already in the first decade PosDis, and evolved later into Surnemalism.

## Surnemalism (Primitive)
This ideology appeared in the 40's opposing the established elite of 1st and 2nd generation.

It admits the merit of some descendents on "tube children", the focus being more in relevant PosDis ancestors and current leaders, with the creation of new surnames. It highly values personal work and despises organisation and leadership as "not real work".

It is the first articulated ideology. Under the name of Charismatic Revolution, it attempts to create a society "really adapted" to the new circumstances, cutting with the PreDis past. Its main ideologist and leader is Carela Lopez Jiang.

It is Socialist, rejecting even the need of money.

Once in power, it provokes the universalisation of the two surnames, the introduction of the custodian thirds and other societal changes.

After the emergence of Militarism, it wanes and is later subsumed in the "modern" Surnemalism.

## Elitism
This ideology emerges in the 80's opposing Surnemalism.

It is basically a return-to-the-past movement, in two senses: the elites which lost influence during the Charismatic Revolution, and the restoration and preservation of the past PreDis including national cultures.

It defends capitalism and marriage.

It survives the whole Militarist primacy, merging slowly with the modern Surnemalism and disappearing definitely with the emergece of Federalism.

## Militarism (Saganism)
This ideology emerges in the 120's , its main proponent being Eric (Walder) Sagan. Fully articulated as such, it will become the most influencial and structured ideology of the following four centuries.

It considers that society must retrun to an extent to the feelings of the first decades. That the main focus must be the "four phases" or "survive, hide, grow, revenge" and it is at that point in the third one.

It is centralistic, remarks the individual duty towards the State.

It defends the creation of Armed Forces, which are focused on the "four phases" and should never, under no circumstances, be used against other humans. This is called "The Horror". "Never Against Humans" is a slogan everybody understands.

It defends republican ideals, also out of fear of what a unique leader can do with the Armed Forces. It is fiercly opposed to corruption in public officials, leaving more leeway to private individuals.

It stands in the middle between Surnemalism and Elitism in some aspects: Surnames are ok, but only one. Recover some aspects of the past, but not nationalism.

## Surnemalism ("Modern")
This ideology emerges between 200 and 250 and is directly descending from Surnemalism, but it has also some some differences.

It defends the importance of some big families and the culture they defend and represent, often associated with old Earth nations, but also with the different "Concesions" and new settlements. This love for diversity connects it with future Federalism.

It develops rituals and ideological elites, later called "priests". In a sense, it becomes a religion.

It accepts the economic consensus established my Militarism, calling out its progressive drift to a central economy around the Armed Forces.

## Federalism
Emerging in the decade of the 300' and fully structured, this is a re-thinking of Elitist and Surnemalist ideals with a new basis: the "Cultural Parallax". It will be the second most important ideology until the arrival of the Exterminating Mother.

This idea defends cultural diversity as way of growth, wisdom and protection both against ETs as well as against "cultural plagues".

It is future-oriented, old Earth nations serving only as vague references or source of inspiration.

## Return
This ideology emerges in the 410's with the impulse to return to Earth, make it habitable again, and make it again the centre of mankind.

Very much oriented to the past, it serves as refuge of discontent Surnemalists (because of religiosity) and Federalists (because of abandonment of the PreDis past). It has (PreDis XIX-Century's) Romantic roots.

There is a one-time alliance with Militarists around 410-430, against Federalists and Surnemalists. This should not provoke confusion with any ideological approach, but it does have a permanent effect on the state (the name of the Armed Forces, for example).

It loses power and influence after 430', but it does not dissapear and will later inspire Brunhild Baubeiter.

## United Diversity mainstream
This ideology is the reaction of the arrival of the Exterminating Mother as it, somehow, replies to the question of who was right in the centuries-long debate between Militarists and Federalists: "both".
